About & privacy

  • WifiScope reads the same Wi-Fi list Windows has (netsh wlan show networks mode=bssid) and asks the adapter for a fresh scan. It never connects to a network, changes a setting, or sends anything over the internet.
  • Nothing is saved to disk except these two settings. Scan results and the signal graph live in memory only.
  • On Windows 11, apps can only see nearby networks when Location services and Let desktop apps access your location are on. WifiScope tells you if that is the problem. It does not use your location for anything.
  • Signal in dBm is an estimate from Windows' percentage (percent ÷ 2 − 100). Channel width and exact transmit power are not available from Windows' text output.
  • Channel advice weighs nearby networks by overlap and strength. It ignores your own router's other radios, so it will not tell you to move away from yourself. Changing channels is done in your router's settings.
